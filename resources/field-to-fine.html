The field-to-fine map.

Every field in the Decision Evidence schema exists because the absence of that record produced an enforcement action. This is the map. It is simultaneously the product's rationale, the sales case, and the spec's justification.

References are to public enforcement actions and are provided for compliance context, not legal advice. Citations are being finalized; treat dates/amounts as subject to verification against the source orders.

Schema fieldWhat it recordsAction its absence producedThe finding, in one line
suppression_justificationThe recorded reason a below-the-line event was not escalated.SEC v. Merrill Lynch, Jun 2026 — $7.5MBelow-threshold alerts suppressed; no record of who reviewed the BTL testing or why.
threshold_version_idWhich threshold policy was in force at decision time.Merrill, 2023Threshold changes not defensibly documented against the decisions they governed.
btl_sampling_flag + ledgerThat the below-the-line population was sampled and dispositioned.Recurring BTL-testing findings across BSA examsNo evidence the suppressed population was ever reviewed as a control.
coverage / feed integrityProof the monitored population was complete.TD, 2024Monitoring gaps meant a segment of activity was never scored.
tuning-cadence recordThat thresholds were reviewed on a defensible schedule.USAAModel/threshold tuning cadence not evidenced.
reviewer + decidedAtWho dispositioned an alert and when.Canaccord and peer BD actionsAlert review under-resourced / unattributable.
recordHash / chainThat a disposition record has not been altered since it was written.Cross-cutting recordkeeping (SEC 17a-8)No integrity guarantee on the evidence produced to examiners.
policyVersionId (continuing activity)The versioned monitoring policy in force, incl. the FinCEN Oct-2025 continuing-activity change.Continuing-activity SAR-timing findingsPolicy version applied to a decision not reconstructable.

The Atestia reference implementation writes every one of these fields per decision, on a tamper-evident hash chain, and renders them into the Examiner Evidence Packet. The teardown of the anchor action is here: the Merrill $7.5M teardown.
